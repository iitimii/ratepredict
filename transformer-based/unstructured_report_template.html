A local inventory combining the canonical dataset, both Data Sources CSVs, the original news aggregator, the original macro calendar, and newer curated NBS resources. Sources without collected text remain visible.

Available material includes document titles, descriptions, URLs, resource metadata, archive indexes, manual holiday ranges, and undated event definitions. Full article/document bodies and embeddings are not available in this report. Recurring schedules have not been expanded into invented historical events.

The daily CSV has every calendar day from 2006-01-01 through 2026-09-02 and every source column. Nonempty cells contain JSON lists of record IDs, resolvable in the all-records CSV/JSON. Blank cells mean no date-aligned local record. Records with only a year, unknown publication dates, or sitemap timestamps remain in the record export rather than being assigned artificial dates.
